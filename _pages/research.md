# Our Research

Our work focuses predominantly on the biological clock. We are interested in different clocks which differ in the period of their oscillation: the **tidal clock** is driven by the gravitational pull of the Sun and Moon on the Earth and peaks twice per lunar day (every 12.4 hours). The **circadian clock** from the Latin "circa", meaning "around", and diem, meaning "day", oscillates with a period of 24 hours, and the **seasonal clock** is driven by the changing seasons and therefore is characterised by a period of one year.
